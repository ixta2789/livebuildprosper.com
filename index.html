What I Do

Areas of Focus

Many high earners and business owners lose more than they realize, not because they aren't earning enough, but because the strategy around their income hasn't kept up with what they've built. We focus on taxes, exits, and retirement because they aren't separate problems. They're the same problem at different stages.

Tax mitigation is where most engagements start. We educate clients on strategies grounded in the existing tax code and coordinate them with your CPA before anything moves forward. Business owners and W-2 earners with roughly $350K+ in income or revenue are typically the best fit.

Business exits reward early planning. The tax exposure on a sale or transition can be significant. It shapes how much you keep and can change which offers make sense. The earlier we start, the more options there are, but planning still matters after a deal closes. Whether you're preparing to sell or already have, we work through timing, structure, and next steps alongside your CPA and attorney.

Retirement optimization is about structuring your accounts to be tax-efficient in retirement and aligned with your full financial picture. It runs alongside tax mitigation, not separate from it.
